The Audio component plays an audio file with our own controls: play/pause, skip back 15 and forward 30 seconds, time, a seek bar, playback speed, mute and volume.
It uses a native `<audio>` element and the unstyled [`a-media-*`](/elements/a-media/) elements, styled with Tailwind and Atrium icons. No player library is required.

## Guidelines

- **Don't autoplay**: Browsers block it and it surprises users. Let them start playback.
- **Provide context**: Show what is playing next to the player, like a title, date and length of an episode.
- **Transcripts**: Link a transcript for recordings with speech, audio has no visible captions.
- **Small screens**: Skip and volume controls are hidden below the `sm` breakpoint, devices have hardware volume controls.
  Times are padded to the length of the duration, so the controls don't shift during playback.

## Accessibility

- **Label**: The `label` prop names the player group for screen readers. Describe the content of the recording.
- **Controls**: All controls are focusable buttons and sliders with accessible names.
- **Keyboard**: <kbd>Enter</kbd>/<kbd>Space</kbd> activate buttons. On the seek bar <kbd>←</kbd>/<kbd>→</kbd> seek 5 seconds, on the volume slider the arrow keys change the volume.
