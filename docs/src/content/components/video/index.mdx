The Video component plays a video file with our own controls: play/pause, time, a seek bar, mute, volume and fullscreen.
State and media handling come from [Video.js](https://videojs.org/) (`<video-player>`, `<media-container>`), the controls are the unstyled [`a-video-*`](/elements/a-video/) elements, styled with Tailwind and Atrium icons.

Controls hide while the video plays and the pointer is idle, and show again on pointer movement, focus or pause.

Requires the `@videojs/html` package.

## Guidelines

- **Don't autoplay with sound**: Browsers block it and it surprises users. If a video has to start on its own, it must be muted.
- **Provide a poster**: It reserves the frame with meaningful content and avoids a black box before the first frame loads.
- **Captions**: Add a `<track kind="captions">` for videos with speech.
- **Layout shifts**: The player reserves its space with an aspect ratio (16/9 by default) before the video or the custom elements load, also when rendered on the server.
  Match it to your video with a class like `aspect-square` or `aspect-[4/5]`, otherwise the video is letterboxed. Times are padded to the length of the duration, so the controls don't shift during playback.
- **Streaming**: For HLS or DASH sources, use a Video.js media element like `<hlsjs-video>` instead of `<video>`.

## Accessibility

- **Label**: The `label` prop names the player for screen readers. Describe the content of the video.
- **Controls**: All controls are focusable buttons and sliders with accessible names. Focusing a control keeps the controls visible.
- **Keyboard**: When the player is focused, <kbd>Space</kbd>/<kbd>K</kbd> toggles playback, <kbd>M</kbd> mutes, <kbd>F</kbd> toggles fullscreen and <kbd>←</kbd>/<kbd>→</kbd> seek 5 seconds.
- **Pointer**: Click the video to play or pause, double click to toggle fullscreen.
